Grandmother's ragù

forked from Nonna Lucia's original

3 hours Serves 6 14 commits

A loose, forgiving sauce that rewards patience more than precision. The version I keep coming back to — adjust the wine to whatever's open.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 2 carrots, finely diced
  • 2 celery ribs, finely diced
  • 150g guanciale, diced
  • 500g beef & pork mince
  • 1 cup whole milk, warmed
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 400g tinned tomatoes
  • handful torn basil

Method

  1. 1

    Soften the soffritto slowly in olive oil — ten unhurried minutes, until everything turns translucent and sweet.

  2. 2

    Add the guanciale and let it render, then raise the heat and brown the mince well, breaking up any clumps.

  3. 3

    Pour in the warmed milk and let it bubble away almost completely latest change

  4. 4

    Add the wine, reduce by half, then the tomatoes. Simmer uncovered for 3 hours, stirring now and then.

  5. 5

    Finish with a handful of torn basil and a long rest off the heat before serving.
