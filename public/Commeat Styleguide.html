A cookbook that remembers

The Commeat design system

Recipes are Markdown files, versioned like code — every edit a commit, every adaptation a fork. This guide keeps that soul intact while the experience stays warm, tactile and personal. Built on tokens you can drop straight into the app.

01

Brand & wordmark

The mark is a version stem with three commit nodes — and a leaf sprouting from the latest, because a recipe is something living that you tend over time. Set the wordmark in Newsreader Medium.

Color

Warm sage grounds the app; paper and cream carry content; forest ink does the reading. Forest green is the single voice of action, clay the warm highlight for forks and new things. Nothing is pure black or white.

Elevation & motion

Shadows are soft and warm-tinted — paper lifting off a counter, never floating in space. Motion is subtle and purposeful: 120–320ms, gentle easing, nothing that performs.

10

The git metaphor, warmly

We keep the real words — commit, fork, diff, branch, history — but dress them in the serif system. Hashes are small-caps serif tags (no terminal), the timeline reads like a family tree, and the diff is softened to warm green & clay.

History — commit timeline

Added a splash of milk to mellow the acidity
v3 · you · 2 days ago
Swapped pancetta for guanciale
v2 · you · 3 weeks ago
Forked from Nonna's original
fork · 2 months ago

Diff — softened

+1 cup whole milk, warmed
−½ cup heavy cream
+Simmer uncovered for 3 hours, stirring now and then
Finish with a handful of torn basil
